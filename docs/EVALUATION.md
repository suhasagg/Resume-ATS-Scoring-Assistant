# Evaluation strategy

Extraction: precision/recall for skills, dates and evidence spans; unsupported claim rate.
Scoring: deterministic reproducibility, taxonomy synonym tests, rubric conformance and formatting invariance.
LLM: groundedness, prompt-injection resistance, no protected-attribute reasoning and provider-upgrade regression.
Product: recruiter correction/override reason distribution and review time.
Fairness: use a separately governed audit process and legally reviewed datasets; protected attributes must not become scoring features.
